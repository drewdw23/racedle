About Racedle

Racedle is a free, browser-based daily guessing game for NASCAR fans — a Wordle-style puzzle where you guess the mystery Cup Series driver from clues about their team, championships, wins, status, and era. It's a fan project, built to be fast, free, and playable on any device with no account and no install.

How it works

Every day, each mode serves one shared mystery driver that every player worldwide gets. You guess driver names and Racedle grades each guess across five categories with color clues until you find the answer. Ultimate draws from every era of the Cup Series, and the decade modes (the 1970s through the 2020s) each draw only from the drivers who debuted in that decade. Every mode has its own daily puzzle, unlimited free-play mode, and personal stats. New to the game? Start with How to Play.

Where the data comes from

Racedle covers every NASCAR Cup Series driver who ran a full-time season (at least 60% of the races) in any season from 1970 onward — including the legends who debuted earlier and raced on into the '70s. The data is compiled from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA): each season's points standings and team entries, and the list of Cup Series champions.

The information Racedle uses — names, teams, championships, win counts, and debut years — are publicly documented facts. Racedle uses no team logos, driver photographs, car numbers, or NASCAR marks.

Accuracy & corrections

The database is large and compiled from public records, so occasional errors are possible. If you spot a driver whose team, title count, or other detail looks wrong, please open an issue on GitHub — corrections are welcome and get fixed quickly.

Privacy

Racedle has no accounts and no server-side database. Your game progress, streaks, and stats live only in your own browser. See the full privacy policy for details.

Disclaimer
