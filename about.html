About Racedle

Racedle is a free, browser-based daily guessing game for motorsports fans — a Wordle-style puzzle where you guess the mystery racing driver from clues about their nationality, series, team, championships, wins, and era. It's a fan project, built to be fast, free, and playable on any device with no account and no install.

How it works

Every day, each genre serves one shared mystery driver that every player worldwide gets. You guess driver names and Racedle grades each guess across seven categories with color clues until you find the answer. There are seven genres — Ultimate (the combined pool) plus Formula 1, NASCAR, IndyCar, V8 Supercars, and Rally (WRC) — each with its own daily puzzle, unlimited free-play mode, and personal stats. New to the game? Start with How to Play.

Where the data comes from

Racedle covers full-time drivers from 1970 onward across five racing series, compiled entirely from open, license-clean public sources:

  • Formula 1 — driver records from F1DB (licensed CC BY 4.0), cross-checked against the open Jolpica-F1 API.
  • NASCAR Cup, IndyCar, V8 Supercars, and WRC — assembled from Wikipedia season pages (CC BY-SA) and Wikidata (CC0).

The information Racedle uses — names, nationalities, teams, championships, and win counts — are publicly documented facts. Racedle uses no team logos, driver photographs, or series wordmarks. Championships and wins reflect each driver's primary series.

Accuracy & corrections

The database is large and compiled from public records, so occasional errors are possible. If you spot a driver whose team, title count, or other detail looks wrong, please open an issue on GitHub — corrections are welcome and get fixed quickly.

Privacy

Racedle has no accounts and no server-side database. Your game progress, streaks, and stats live only in your own browser. See the full privacy policy for details.
