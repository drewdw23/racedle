How to Play Racedle

Racedle is a free daily guessing game for NASCAR fans. Each day, guess the mystery NASCAR Cup driver in as few tries as you can — every guess reveals color-coded clues that steer you toward the answer. No account, no download, unlimited guesses.

The objective

A new mystery driver is chosen every day for each mode. Type a driver's name and submit it. Racedle compares your guess to the hidden answer across six categories and colors each one to tell you how close you are. Keep guessing — using the clues — until you find the exact driver.

What the colors mean

  • Green — exact match on that category.
  • Orange — close: the same continent, or one step off on a numeric clue.
  • Gray — not a match.
  • ▲ / ▼ arrows — on the number clues, the arrow points toward the answer: ▲ means the answer is higher, ▼ means it is lower.

The six clues

CategoryWhat it tells you
NationalityThe driver's country. same country · same continent.
TeamTheir current team, or their last team if retired.
TitlesCup Series championships won. = off by one; the ▲▼ arrow points toward the answer.
WinsCareer Cup wins, shown as a band — 0, 1–9, 10–24, 25–49, 50–99, 100+. = one band away.
StatusActive (currently racing full-time) or Retired.
DebutThe decade of their Cup debut (e.g. 1990s). = an adjacent decade.

Daily vs. Free play

  • Daily — one shared puzzle per mode per day. Everyone worldwide gets the same mystery driver, and your progress is saved so you can finish later.
  • Free play — unlimited random rounds for practice. Hit "New driver" for another. Free play doesn't affect your daily streak.

Modes

Every mode has its own daily puzzle, free play, and stats — pick the era you know best, or play them all:

  • 🏆 Ultimate — every driver in the game, from every era.
  • Decade modes — 1950s–60s, 1970s, 1980s, 1990s, 2000s, 2010s and 2020s. Each uses only the drivers whose Cup debut falls in that decade, so the Debut clue is already solved — the other five clues do the work.

Streaks & the daily reset

A fresh daily driver drops at midnight UTC in every mode. Solve the daily puzzle on consecutive days to build a streak. Your stats — games played, win rate, current and best streak, and average guesses — are tracked separately for each mode and stored privately in your own browser (see the privacy policy).

Strategy tips

  • Open with a driver you know well — a green or orange clue anywhere narrows the field fast.
  • Use the ▲▼ arrows on titles, wins, and debut to bracket the answer's era and success level.
  • Team is the sharpest clue: a green team cuts the field to that organization's drivers.
  • Most Cup drivers are American, so an orange or gray nationality is a big hint — think of the series' international regulars.
