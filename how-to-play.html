How to Play Racedle

Racedle is a free daily guessing game for motorsports fans. Each day, guess the mystery racing driver in as few tries as you can — every guess reveals color-coded clues that steer you toward the answer. No account, no download, unlimited guesses.

The objective

A new mystery driver is chosen every day for each genre. Type a driver's name and submit it. Racedle compares your guess to the hidden answer across seven categories and colors each one to tell you how close you are. Keep guessing — using the clues — until you find the exact driver.

What the colors mean

  • Green — exact match on that category.
  • Orange — close: the same continent, or one step off on a numeric clue.
  • Gray — not a match.
  • ▲ / ▼ arrows — on the number clues, the arrow points toward the answer: ▲ means the answer is higher, ▼ means it is lower.

The seven clues

CategoryWhat it tells you
NationalityThe driver's country. same country · same continent.
SeriesTheir primary series: Formula 1, NASCAR Cup, IndyCar, V8 Supercars, or WRC (rally).
TeamTheir current team, or their last team if retired.
TitlesChampionships won in their primary series. = off by one; the ▲▼ arrow points toward the answer.
WinsCareer wins, shown as a band — 0, 1–9, 10–24, 25–49, 50–99, 100+. = one band away.
StatusActive (currently racing full-time) or Retired.
DebutThe decade of their top-level debut (e.g. 1990s). = an adjacent decade.

Daily vs. Free play

  • Daily — one shared puzzle per genre per day. Everyone worldwide gets the same mystery driver, and your progress is saved so you can finish later.
  • Free play — unlimited random rounds for practice. Hit "New driver" for another. Free play doesn't affect your daily streak.

Genres

Every genre has its own daily puzzle, free play, and stats — pick the racing you know best, or play them all:

  • 🏆 Ultimate — the full pool, drawing from every series below.
  • Formula 1, NASCAR, IndyCar, V8 Supercars, and Rally (WRC) — each its own single-series puzzle.

Streaks & the daily reset

A fresh daily driver drops at midnight UTC in every genre. Solve the daily puzzle on consecutive days to build a streak. Your stats — games played, win rate, current and best streak, and average guesses — are tracked separately for each genre and stored privately in your own browser (see the privacy policy).

Strategy tips

  • Open with a driver you know well — a green or orange clue anywhere narrows the field fast.
  • Use the ▲▼ arrows on titles, wins, and debut to bracket the answer's era and success level.
  • An orange nationality means "right continent, wrong country" — switch to a driver from a neighboring racing nation.
  • In Ultimate, the Series clue is gold: once it turns green you only need to search within that series.
