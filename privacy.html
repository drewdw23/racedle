Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Racedle is a free browser game with no accounts and no server-side database. This policy explains the small amount of data involved when you play.

What we store

Racedle stores your game progress and statistics (games played, win streaks, guess history for the current daily puzzle) in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and we have no way to read it. Clearing your browser data erases it.

Advertising & third-party cookies

This site may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. When ads are enabled:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • For more on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent prompt (a Google-certified consent management platform) before any non-essential advertising cookies are set, in line with GDPR.

Analytics

Racedle does not run its own analytics or tracking scripts. Any measurement is limited to what the advertising provider above collects to serve and measure ads.

Children

Racedle is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves; the "last updated" date above reflects the most recent change.

Contact

Questions or data concerns? Please open an issue on GitHub.
